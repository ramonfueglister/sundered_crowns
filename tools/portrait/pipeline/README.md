# The pipeline of the real look's files

Apoleia's `tools/portrait-pipeline` (Blender scripts that drape clothes over MakeHuman's body, lay hair cards, and
bind both to the head's triangles so the builder can move them with a morphed head), with what the dress of England
1356 needed added: `tunic_sim.py` (neck opening `NECK`, `NDIP`; sleeves `SLEN`, `SFLARE`; since round 110 a neckline cut along a line, `NECKLINE`, `NDIPW`, `neckline.py`, a garment that is a yoke alone, `YMIN`, `SLEEVES=0`, and one that lies on the skin without a drape, `ONSKIN`), `drape_under.py` (round 110: a gown draped anew held under the gown it takes the place of), `drape_sim.py` (kinds
`hood` and `cowl`; `UCUT`, `CHIN`, `THROAT`, `THMAX`, `OFF0`, `FACE_ON`, `EARS`, `VTH`, `SK_OUT`, `CW_IN`, `CW_UP`, `CW_GAP`, and since round 163 `CW_DIP`, `CW_RO`, `CW_INP`, `CW_U0`, `CW_HOLD`, `COWL`'s fifth value), `styles.py` (hairstyles `bob` and `plait`; `bob` with `BOB_CUT`: every strand is cut where it crosses the fringe's line in front of the face),
`tunic_bind.py` (`HEAD`, `HEM`, `HEMSMOOTH`, `HEMFOLLOW`, `HEM_AO`, `TAUBIN`, `HEMX`, `HEMY`; the hem's tube welded to the cloth's edge), `fabric_tex.py` (woven cloth as an image; `NAP`, `DEPTH`, `MOTT`),
`skin_lash.py` (the lash line on the lid's edge), `skin_lip.py` (the red of the lips along their outline).
`skin_red.py` is Apoleia's (where the skin flushes), with its zones as switches since round 71.

Not part of the game's build: the files they wrote are in `public/portrait/real/`. This folder is there so that
they can be written again.

Needs: `python3 -m venv venv && venv/bin/pip install bpy==5.2.3 numpy pillow` (PY below), and MakeHuman's body
`base.obj` (github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman, makehuman/data/3dobjs/base.obj, CC0; B below).
